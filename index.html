Aika Labsaikalabs.ai

Aika means “listen” in Marathi.

Most of India is left out of today’s AI. Apps and chatbots assume a smartphone, a data connection, typing and English. Aika needs none of that: you call a number and talk.

She helps with everyday questions, answers in your language, and is happy to just listen. Making technology work for the people it usually leaves out is what I want to spend my life on.

About

I’ve spent my career building large distributed systems and, more recently, AI products and the infrastructure under them.

At Amazon I started as an intern and grew into an engineering lead for the AI Shopping Experience team serving Emerging Stores. Along the way I pitched and got funding for a secure data-sharing platform for third parties. It grew into a small team of its own, serves 270+ external clients and still powers Amazon India’s accounting. I also led privacy compliance automation across 350+ services, and the catalog schema work that cut adding a new product attribute from a year to a week.

In 2026 I joined Auxia as a Staff Engineer, then went full time on Aika in September. I’m also doing an online M.S. in AI at UT Austin.

Outside work, I sing and write songs on guitar and piano.
