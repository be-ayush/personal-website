About

Engineer turned founder.

I’ve spent my career building large-scale distributed systems and, more recently, AI products and the infrastructure behind them.

At Amazon I joined as an intern and grew into an engineering lead for the AI Shopping Experience team serving Emerging Stores. I proposed and secured funding for a secure data-sharing platform for third parties, which grew into a small startup inside the company and still powers Amazon India’s accounting.

I then joined Auxia, an AI marketing scale-up, as a Staff Engineer, before going full time on Aika in September 2026. I’m also studying for an online M.S. in AI at UT Austin.

Away from work, I sing and write songs, on guitar and piano.
