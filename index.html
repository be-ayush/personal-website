Now

Aika

In Marathi, aika means “listen.”

Hundreds of millions of people are left behind by apps and chatbots built for screens, typing and English. Aika is a phone number they can call and talk to in their own language, for everyday help, answers and a patient listener. No smartphone, no internet, no reading required.

It is my life’s work: bringing frontier technology to people in India and beyond who have historically been underserved by it.

About

I’ve spent my career building large-scale distributed systems and, more recently, AI products and the infrastructure behind them.

Before Aika, I spent over eight years at Amazon, joining as an intern and growing into an engineering lead for the AI Shopping Experience team serving Emerging Stores. Along the way I proposed and secured funding for a secure data-sharing platform for third parties, which grew into a small startup inside the company and still powers Amazon India’s accounting. I also led privacy compliance automation across 350+ services. After Amazon I joined Auxia, an AI marketing scale-up, as a Staff Engineer leading its offline delivery platform.

Away from work, I sing and write songs, on guitar and piano.
